# Buffett-Style Investment Memo: COST

**Verdict: own the business**

COST shows the kind of operating record a long-term owner can understand and
respect. ROE remained strong at 28.3%, 25.1%, 31.2%, 27.8%, and 25.8%. Net
income rose from $5,844 million at the 2022-08-28 fiscal year end to $9,226
million at the 2026-08-30 fiscal year end.

Owner earnings were positive in every reported year. They increased from
$3,501 million to $9,382 million, despite a modest movement from $6,745 million
to $6,629 million in the middle of the record. The Python results therefore
show all three Buffett checks as pass.

The record supports owning the business on quality grounds: consistently high
returns on equity, positive owner earnings, and substantially higher owner
earnings at the end of the period. This verdict judges the operating business
only and makes no claim about its market price.

## My Decision

I would trust the Costco verdict because the company consistently maintained strong ROE and positive owner earnings over the five-year period. However, I would still consider other factors before making a final investment decision.
