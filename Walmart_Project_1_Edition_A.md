## Consequential Assumptions

The valuation will depend heavily on assumptions about future revenue growth, operating margins, capital expenditures, working capital needs, WACC, and the terminal growth rate. Small changes to these assumptions could materially change the estimated equity value.

## Unknowns

I still need to determine whether Walmart's recent growth rate is sustainable, how margins may change over the forecast period, how much reinvestment will be required to support growth, and whether the resulting intrinsic value supports initiating coverage.

## Research Plan

I will review Walmart's financial statements and most recent quarterly results, develop a five-year operating forecast, calculate FCFF, estimate WACC, calculate terminal value, and complete the enterprise-to-equity bridge. I will then run sensitivity analysis on the assumptions that have the largest effect on the valuation.
